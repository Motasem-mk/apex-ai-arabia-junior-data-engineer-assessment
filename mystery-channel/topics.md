# Mystery channel — first 50 topics

English-language channel for US, UK and Canadian viewers. Every topic is a real, documented case. Topics about violent crime against identifiable recent victims are left out on purpose: they limit advertising and carry legal risk.

"Check" marks a topic whose details came from a single or weak source and must be verified against the original study or agency statement before a script is written.
